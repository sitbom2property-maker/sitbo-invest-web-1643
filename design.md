# Sitbo Invest — Design System

## Brand
Premium real estate investment agency in Batumi, Georgia. Inspired by letusibiza.com.
Editorial luxury. Trust through restraint. Gold signals exclusivity, not excess.

## Color Palette
- `--color-navy`: #0A1628 — deep sea blue (dominant background)
- `--color-slate`: #1E2D40 — mid-tone dark surface
- `--color-cream`: #F5F0E8 — warm off-white (primary text background light)
- `--color-gold`: #C9A84C — gold accent
- `--color-gold-light`: #E8C97A — hover gold
- `--color-muted`: #8A96A3 — muted text / secondary
- `--color-white`: #FFFFFF
- `--color-border`: rgba(201,168,76,0.2) — gold border subtle

## Typography
- Display: `Cormorant Garamond` (serif, editorial luxury) — headlines
- Body: `DM Sans` (clean, modern sans) — body copy, UI
- Accent/Label: `DM Sans` uppercase tracking-widest

## Spacing
Generous. Sections: py-24 to py-32. Hero: min-h-screen.

## Motion
- Scroll-triggered fade-in-up on sections
- Hover scale/lift on property cards
- Smooth scroll behavior
- Subtle parallax on hero

## Layout
- Large editorial headlines (like letusibiza.com)
- Asymmetric grids, right-side image clusters in hero
- Full-bleed sections alternating dark navy and cream
- Horizontal scrollable portfolio cards (inspired by reference image 2)

## Components
- Property card: image fills card, title/desc overlay, gold CTA pill, hover lifts + reveals more info
- Stat tiles: large gold number, small label
- Form: dark background, gold focus ring, minimal fields
